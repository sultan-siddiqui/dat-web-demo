Understand your numbers

Four terms worth knowing.

APR

The annual percentage rate shows the yearly cost of finance, including profit, so you can compare offers fairly.

Loan to value

How much of the property price we finance. 90% means a 10% down payment.

Grace period

Optional, up to 6 months: your first payment starts after you get your home.

Early payment charge

If you settle early, the charge is the profit on your next three instalments.
