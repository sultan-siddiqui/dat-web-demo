Home Finance Murabaha

Fixed from the first instalment to the last.

A Shariah-compliant home finance program based on the Murabaha structure, for employees, retirees and residents. It supports home ownership in line with Saudi Vision 2030 through flexible, reliable and easy financing.

  • Shariah compliant
  • Fixed profit rate
  • Deed in your name

One contract, one price, one instalment. You always know exactly what you owe and exactly when it ends.

This program is designed to increase home ownership among citizens and residents, in line with Saudi Vision 2030's goals for the housing sector. We combine flexibility, transparency and an easy process so you can achieve home ownership with confidence.

  • Fixed rate, fixed payment (Murabaha)
  • No salary transfer: work with any bank
  • The deed is in the customer's name
  • Financing up to 30 years
  • Financing up to age 75
  • 15% down payment for second-home buyers
  • REDF & MOH beneficiaries included
  • Shariah compliant
  • All government and private sectors
  • Up to 6 months grace period, optional
Co-applicant: husband and wife can apply together

Required documents

Everything to bring, in one list.

Your Home Finance Specialist may ask for more once they review your application.

Masakin's fatwa. The program is authorised by the Dar Al Tamleek Shariah Board. Read the fatwa (PDF, Arabic)

  • Completed application form, signed
  • Saudi National ID and family ID to verify dependents, signed

  • Salary letter on employer letterhead with authorised signature, showing hire date, current position and status, monthly or annual salary and any bonus income (may be addressed to the Public Pension Agency), dated within 60 days of the contract date
  • Bank statements for the last 6 months showing consistent income transfers
  • Direct debit form with signature verified by the bank
  • Masakin program application fee of SAR 200

  • Documentation of the nature of the income
  • Any documents clarifying the additional income, such as lease contracts
  • Bank statements for the last 2 years showing the additional income

  • The seller's offer and agreement to sell, signed by the seller (with mobile number) and by the customer to verify price and property
  • Copy of the building licence. Its date sets the property age unless a later completion certificate or first electricity receipt is provided
  • Copy of the property deed signed by the seller
  • Property location map
  • Copy of the property deed signed by the customer
  • Property inspection report
