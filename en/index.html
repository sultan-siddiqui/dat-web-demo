Murabaha, made simple

Four steps. No interest. No surprises.

Murabaha is a sale, not a loan. Here is exactly what happens between choosing your home and holding its keys.

1of 4 · How Murabaha works

01

You choose the home.

Find the villa, apartment or building you want, from any seller or developer. Get pre-qualified first so you know your budget.

  • Pre-qualification in seconds
  • Independent property appraisal

02

Dar Al Tamleek buys it.

We purchase the property from the seller. For a moment, we own it, which is what makes the contract a genuine sale and not a loan.

  • A real asset changes hands
  • No riba (interest) involved

03

We sell it to you at a fixed price.

You know the purchase cost and our profit up front. Together they make one fixed price, and the title deed goes straight into your name.

  • Full disclosure of cost and profit
  • Deed in your name from day one

04

You pay the same amount every month.

Spread the price over up to 30 years in equal instalments. Market rates can move; your instalment never does.

  • Fixed instalment for the whole term
  • Optional grace period of up to 6 months

Murabaha or Ijarah? With Murabaha the property is registered in your name immediately and your profit rate is fixed. With Ijarah you rent until the end of the term. Compare both
