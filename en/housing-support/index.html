REDF & MOH beneficiaries

Your housing support, built in.

The REDF and MOH Sponsorship Housing Support Program covers your home finance with Dar Al Tamleek. Add your support to your Murabaha finance and lower both your down payment and your monthly cost.

  • 5% down payment
  • 35–100% of profit covered
  • Paid monthly to you

An updated housing subsidy that pays part of your financing cost every month, so owning costs less from day one.

The Masakin (Murabaha) home finance program is provided by the General Organization for Social Insurance in cooperation with Dar Al Tamleek, which services the program. All beneficiaries can add their housing support to the finance.

It is open to all eligible beneficiaries of the Real Estate Development Fund or the Ministry of Housing, employed in the government or private sector, and all retirees.

How the support works

Three steps, every month.

01

You get home finance from Dar Al Tamleek

Program support begins once your home finance is in place.

02

The Fund covers 35% to 100% of the profit

The exact share follows the approved support matrix for your profile.

03

Support lands in your account

It is transferred directly to you after each monthly instalment is paid.

Support matrix

Between 35% and all of your profit.

The Real Estate Development Fund and the Ministry of Housing pay a monthly amount that covers your financing profit according to the approved product matrix.

Housing support, explained.

Masakin fatwa. The REDF Subsidised Lending Program works with GOSI's Masakin program, administered by Dar Al Tamleek. See our fatwas

It was designed to encourage home ownership among Saudi citizens. Beneficiaries receive a monthly payment to cover the profit on up to SAR 500,000 of the financed amount.

Beneficiaries provide a 5% down payment instead of the 10% SAMA requires for standard real estate financing. REDF guarantees the other 5% of the property value. The maximum property value is SAR 800,000.

REDF's Home Lending Program works with GOSI's Masakin program, administered by Dar Al Tamleek, to offer government-supported home finance to approved beneficiaries in the government and private sectors and all retirees.

A minimum of SAR 150,000 and a maximum of SAR 5,000,000.

An independent appraisal company determines the value of the property.

Completed villas, duplexes, townhomes and apartments up to 20 years old. Apartment buildings of up to 10 units qualify if you live in one of them. Second homes qualify too.
